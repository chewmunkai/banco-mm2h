FACTUAL — wrong district
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
孟沙 is Bangsar. Mont Kiara is a different Kuala Lumpur neighbourhood entirely
(蒙特奇亞拉 / 满家乐 in Malaysian Chinese). Naming the wrong district under a
photograph of it is a credibility problem for a property advisory, so the Latin
name stays — it is also how a Taiwanese buyer will search for it.

Mont Kiara · 住宅區

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TYPOGRAPHY — no spaces between CJK characters
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Chinese does not space around inline elements. Every <span> and <a> boundary in
these strings had left a gap mid-sentence (的 途徑, 問 談談, 詳見 房產與產權).
Latin runs still take a space on each side — that rule is unchanged.
