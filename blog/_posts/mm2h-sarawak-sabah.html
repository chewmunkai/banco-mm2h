MM2H in Sarawak is a separate programme, S-MM2H, run by the Sarawak government rather than MOTAC. Since 2025 it asks for a RM 500,000 fixed deposit in a Sarawak bank from age 30, with 30 days a year in the state. That's according to Invest Sarawak, whose own page still lists the older rules alongside the new ones. Sabah has run its own state MM2H since July 2024, but has published no official criteria page we could find. This guide sets out what each state has said, where the sources disagree, and how both compare with federal MM2H.

Key takeaways

  • Sarawak S-MM2H, 2025 rules: age 30+, a RM 500,000 fixed deposit in a Sarawak bank, 30 days a year in Sarawak, and a 5-year renewable pass (Invest Sarawak).
  • The same official page still shows the older rules (age 50+ or 30–49, RM 150,000 deposit plus RM 7,000 a month income, 10-year pass). Malay Mail reported the tightening from 1 January 2025, so the 2025 set is most likely current. Confirm before you apply.
  • Sabah MM2H: Sabah's own scheme since July 2024, revised in December 2024. Reported: age 30+, a high-rise home of at least RM 600,000, valid only in Sabah, no work. Reported deposit figures conflict.
  • Federal MM2H is MOTAC's programme. Because Sabah and Sarawak control their own immigration, check with your licensed agent that a federal pass covers living there.
  • Sarawak's RM 2M route to settle permanently, announced in 2024, is a separate scheme, not MM2H.

MM2H Sarawak requirements: what Invest Sarawak says

Invest Sarawak's page on the S-MM2H requirements, dated 23 April 2026, lists two different sets of rules. We show both, because an applicant reading that page will see both.

Separate scheme In April 2024 Sarawak's Premier said investors wanting to migrate to Sarawak must deposit at least RM 2 million or buy property worth RM 1.5 million (The Star, 28 April 2024). That's a state route to settling, not MM2H. MM2H, federal or state, is a long-term visit pass, not permanent residency.

MM2H Sabah requirements: what's actually known

Sabah opened its own MM2H programme to applications on 1 July 2024 (FMT, 30 June 2024) and revised it in December 2024 (The Star, 12 December 2024). From those reports:

  • Applicants must be at least 30.
  • They must buy a high-rise home worth at least RM 600,000.
  • The pass is valid only within Sabah, and it doesn't allow work.

We found no official Sabah criteria page. The fixed-deposit figures quoted online conflict: agent and vendor sites give anything from RM 150,000 to RM 500,000. Don't pay anyone for a "Borneo visa" until the Sabah state government's current criteria are in writing.

Sabah's floor for foreign buyers outside the scheme is reported at RM 600,000 for condos and RM 1M for landed homes. In 2016 the state said it would keep its RM 1M minimum (The Star, 18 November 2016). The RM 600,000 condo figure comes from portal sources.

Can you use federal MM2H in Sabah or Sarawak?

Sabah and Sarawak keep their own control over immigration under the terms on which they joined Malaysia. Sarawak and Sabah each now offer their own MM2H. If you hold, or plan to hold, a federal MM2H pass and want to live in either state, ask your licensed agent in writing whether the pass covers it before you buy a home there.

Frequently asked questions

What are the MM2H Sarawak requirements for 2026?

Under the 2025 rules on Invest Sarawak's page: age 30 or over, a RM 500,000 fixed deposit in a Sarawak bank, 30 days a year in Sarawak, and a 5-year renewable pass. The same page also shows older rules, so confirm with the state before applying.

Is S-MM2H the same as MM2H?

No. S-MM2H is run by the Sarawak government, with its own deposit (in ringgit, in a Sarawak bank), age and stay rules. Federal MM2H is run by MOTAC.

Does Sabah have its own MM2H?

Yes, since July 2024, revised in December 2024. Reports say it requires age 30+, a high-rise home of at least RM 600,000, and is valid only in Sabah. We found no official criteria page, and reported deposit figures conflict.

What is the "Borneo visa"?

It isn't an official name. Agents use it for Sabah's or Sarawak's state MM2H. Check which programme is meant, and get the criteria from the state government.

Can MM2H lead to permanent residency in Sarawak?

No. MM2H is a long-term visit pass. Sarawak's Premier described a separate route in 2024 for investors depositing RM 2 million or buying RM 1.5 million of property. Ask the state for its current terms.

Can I live in Kuching or Kota Kinabalu on federal MM2H?

Ask your licensed agent in writing. Sabah and Sarawak control their own immigration and now run their own programmes.
