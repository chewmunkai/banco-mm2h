Living in Kuala Lumpur costs a family of four about RM 8,600 a month before rent, and a single person about RM 2,350, according to Numbeo's November 2026 figures. Rent adds RM 1,400 to RM 5,100. For a foreigner on MM2H, the bigger number is the home you must buy. In Kuala Lumpur the floor for foreign buyers is a reported RM 1 million. Cross the city line into Petaling Jaya, Subang or Cyberjaya, all in Selangor, and it's RM 2 million. This guide covers the monthly budget, the areas, and how to check which side of the line a home is on.

Key takeaways

  • Monthly costs without rent (Numbeo, Nov 2026): RM 2,349 for a single person, RM 8,571 for a family of four.
  • Rent: RM 1,421 (1-bed, outside the centre) to RM 5,138 (3-bed, city centre).
  • Property floor: RM 1M in Kuala Lumpur. RM 2M in Selangor zones 1–2, which include Petaling Jaya, Subang Jaya, Shah Alam and Cyberjaya's district.
  • For MM2H Silver, that means a home of at least RM 1M in KL, not the tier's RM 600,000, and RM 2M just over the border.
  • Check the address, not the marketing. "KL" in a listing doesn't mean the Federal Territory of Kuala Lumpur.

Why the same Silver visa costs RM 1M in KL and RM 2M in Petaling Jaya

Land is a state matter in Malaysia, and Kuala Lumpur (a federal territory) and Selangor set different floors for foreign buyers. For an MM2H buyer, the price that applies is the higher of the tier minimum and the state floor.

Stamp duty for foreign buyers is reported at a flat 8% from 1 January 2026 (KPMG's Budget 2026 alert; some sources describe a 4–8% range). Kuala Lumpur has no state levy of the kind Penang and Johor charge, so a RM 1M KL condo costs about RM 1.08M before legal and consent fees.

A listing that says "KL" may be in Selangor. Petaling Jaya, Subang and Cyberjaya are often marketed as Kuala Lumpur. Ask your lawyer to check the title's state before you pay a booking fee. It's a RM 1M difference.

MM2H requirements in Kuala Lumpur

There are no KL-specific MM2H rules. The federal tiers apply: from age 25, a fixed deposit of USD 150,000 (Silver), USD 500,000 (Gold) or USD 1,000,000 (Platinum), a home bought after approval and kept for 10 years, and 90 days a year in Malaysia. Only Platinum allows work. See MM2H Silver vs Gold vs Platinum for the details.

How we'd plan a KL move

Banco's view Our judgement, not an official rule.

  • Rent in the area before you buy. Traffic, not distance, decides how far a school or office feels.
  • If you want Petaling Jaya but not RM 2M, look at Bangsar, Mont Kiara or Desa ParkCity first. They're in KL at RM 1M.
  • In KL, Silver and Gold need the same RM 1M home. The choice between them is the deposit (USD 150,000 vs 500,000) and the pass length (5 vs 15 years).
  • Budget the school fees first if you have children. For many families they cost more than the rent.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to live in Kuala Lumpur?

Numbeo's November 2026 figures put monthly costs without rent at about RM 2,349 for a single person and RM 8,571 for a family of four. With rent, that's roughly RM 3,800 to 5,100 for a single person and RM 11,000 to 13,700 for a family, before school fees.

How much do you need to live comfortably in Kuala Lumpur?

It depends on rent and schooling. On Numbeo's figures, a single person in a city-centre 1-bedroom spends about RM 5,100 a month, and a family of four in a 3-bedroom outside the centre about RM 11,000, before international school fees.

Where do most expats live in Kuala Lumpur?

Mont Kiara, Sri Hartamas, KLCC, Bangsar, Damansara Heights and Desa ParkCity are popular in KL itself. Petaling Jaya, Subang Jaya and Cyberjaya are popular too, but they're in Selangor, where the floor for foreign buyers is RM 2M.

Can foreigners buy property in Kuala Lumpur?

Yes, with state consent, from a reported minimum of RM 1 million for condos and landed homes.

What are the MM2H requirements for Kuala Lumpur?

The same as anywhere in Peninsular Malaysia: age 25+, a USD 150,000 fixed deposit for Silver, and a home bought after approval. In KL the home must be at least RM 1 million, the state floor, even for Silver.

Why is Selangor property more expensive for foreigners?

Selangor's floor for foreign buyers is RM 2 million in zones 1 and 2, which cover most of the Klang Valley outside KL, against RM 1 million in Kuala Lumpur. It has been in force since the state's 2014 rules.
