Retire in Penang, Malaysia: the visa, island vs mainland property, and the 3% levy (2026)

Penang has no retirement visa of its own. Foreign retirees use the federal MM2H pass and then meet Penang's own property rules, which differ sharply between the island and the mainland. Here is what each costs, and which figures are still unconfirmed.

To retire in Penang, Malaysia, most foreigners use the federal MM2H pass: there's no separate Penang retirement visa. The visa is the same anywhere in Peninsular Malaysia. What changes in Penang is the home you must buy. Foreign buyers face a reported floor of RM 1 million for a condo on the island and RM 3 million for a landed house, plus a 3% state levy. The mainland (Seberang Perai) is reported to be cheaper, but those figures conflict. This guide sets out each one and tells you which to confirm before you budget.

Key takeaways

  • The visa: federal MM2H. Silver needs a USD 150,000 fixed deposit and gives a 5-year pass. Minimum age 25, and 90 days a year in Malaysia. It is not permanent residency.
  • Island property (reported): RM 1M for a condo, RM 3M for landed. So Silver's RM 600,000 minimum doesn't apply on the island. The state floor does.
  • Mainland property (unconfirmed): portals report RM 500,000 to RM 750,000 for a condo and RM 1M for landed. Don't budget on these until a Penang lawyer confirms them.
  • On top of the price: a 3% Penang levy (reported) and foreign buyers' stamp duty, reported at a flat 8% from 1 January 2026. A RM 1M island condo costs about RM 1.11M before legal fees.
  • Renting first: George Town rents run from about RM 1,500 for a 1-bedroom outside the centre (Numbeo, November 2026).

Is there a Penang retirement visa?

No. Penang doesn't run its own scheme, unlike Sarawak and Sabah. Foreign retirees in Penang use the federal Malaysia My Second Home (MM2H) pass, run by MOTAC. Its rules are the same whether you live in George Town or Kuala Lumpur.

Every MM2H tier requires you to buy a home after approval and keep it for 10 years. In Penang, that's where the state rules come in.

What a Silver retiree actually pays in Penang

For an MM2H buyer, the price that applies is the higher of the tier minimum and the state floor. On the island, the state floor wins for Silver and Gold. On the mainland, Silver's own RM 600,000 minimum may be what you pay, if the lower reported floor is right.

Island or mainland: how to choose

Banco's view Our judgement, not an official rule.

  • The island is where George Town, the beaches, the airport (Bayan Lepas) and most private hospitals are. You pay for it: at least RM 1M for a condo, and a landed house is out of reach for all but Platinum budgets.
  • The mainland is quieter and cheaper, and two bridges connect it to the island. If the lower floor is confirmed, it's the only part of Penang where Silver's RM 600,000 might still be the real minimum.
  • Choose Silver vs Gold on deposit and pass length, not property. On the island, Silver and Gold both need the same RM 1M condo. Gold only buys you 15 years between renewals for an extra USD 350,000 on deposit.

How to retire in Penang, step by step

  1. Visit and rent first to decide between island and mainland.
  2. Choose your MM2H tier and apply through a MOTAC-licensed agent.
  3. After approval, place the fixed deposit in a Malaysian bank.
  4. Confirm the floor and levy for the exact property with a Penang conveyancing lawyer, then sign the sale agreement with state consent as a condition.
  5. Complete the purchase. Under MM2H, you can't sell the home for 10 years.

Frequently asked questions

Can a foreigner retire in Penang, Malaysia?

Yes. Most foreign retirees use the federal MM2H pass, which gives 5, 15 or 20 years depending on the tier. Silver, the entry tier, needs a USD 150,000 fixed deposit and a home in Malaysia.

Is there a Penang retirement visa?

No. Penang has no scheme of its own. Retirees use MM2H, whose rules are the same across Peninsular Malaysia. Only Penang's property rules differ.

Can foreigners buy property in Penang?

Yes, with state consent. The reported floors are RM 1 million for a condo and RM 3 million for a landed home on the island. The mainland is reported at RM 500,000 to 750,000 for condos and RM 1 million for landed, but those figures are unconfirmed. A 3% levy is reported on top.

What does MM2H cost in Penang?

The MM2H fees and deposit are the same everywhere. The difference is the home: on Penang Island, a Silver applicant must buy at RM 1 million or more, not RM 600,000. With the levy and stamp duty, that's about RM 1.11 million before legal fees.

How much does it cost to rent in George Town?

Numbeo's November 2026 figures put a 1-bedroom at about RM 1,500 a month outside the centre and RM 3,655 in the centre, and a 3-bedroom at RM 3,954 to 4,525. They're crowd-sourced, so check current listings.

Should I retire on Penang Island or the mainland?

The island has George Town, the beaches, the airport and most hospitals, at a higher floor. The mainland is cheaper and quieter but its foreign-buyer floor is unconfirmed. Rent on both sides before you buy.
